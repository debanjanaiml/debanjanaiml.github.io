## 🎯 Career Focus & Philosophy

My name is **Debanjan Saha**, and I am a seasoned technologist specializing in **Machine Learning, Generative AI, and Data Engineering**. With over a decade of experience, I design and deploy large-scale ML and data solutions across multi-cloud environments (AWS, Azure, GCP).

I currently serve as a **Senior Data Engineer** at **Mastercard**. Here, I architect and optimize high-volume data pipelines in **Azure Databricks**, handling up to **45 TB of daily transaction data** to power advanced customer analytics and machine learning applications. My primary focus is driving **data efficiency**, **cost optimization** (e.g., using Terraform and Photon compute), and ensuring high **scalability** and **compliance** (PCI-DSS, GDPR) across critical workflows.

My goal is to advance into a **Lead Machine Learning Engineer** role, where I can leverage my expertise in foundation model development and scalable AI infrastructure. I am passionate about transforming complex, raw data into intelligent, measurable business value through robust **MLOps pipelines** and next-generation systems like **Text-to-SQL LLMs** (which I'm prototyping). I aim to continuously evolve as a leader, driving innovation, efficiency, and the responsible adoption of AI technology.
